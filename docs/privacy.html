Add-in Session Inbox (Chrome extension) · Last updated 19 October 2026

What the extension reads

  • In the Claude add-in panel inside PowerPoint and Outlook for the web (pivot.claude.ai): whether Claude is working, waiting for you, or done, and the first ~200 characters of Claude's last reply.
  • On PowerPoint for the web pages (*.sharepoint.com): the deck's file address, used only for the "Open in desktop app" button.
  • Tab titles and addresses (via the tabs permission), to list and switch to your sessions.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Everything is stored in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) and stays on your device. The extension makes no network requests, has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and shares nothing with the developer or any third party. Rows you mark as reviewed are deleted after 7 days; removing a row or uninstalling the extension deletes its data.

Permissions

  • sidePanel: show the session list.
  • tabs: find, focus and reopen the tabs of your sessions.
  • storage: keep the list on your device.
  • notifications: tell you when a session is done or needs input.
